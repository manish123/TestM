# What is Purnima?

Purnima is the fifteenth Tithi of Shukla Paksha, marking the full-moon portion of the lunisolar month. It is a calendrical Tithi, not merely a visual description of the Moon.

## Why does Purnima exist?

Purnima provides a recurring lunar-calendar boundary and is associated with many regional festivals, Vratas and religious observances.

## How is Purnima calculated?

Purnima is determined through the Tithi system from the angular relationship of the Sun and Moon. The exact beginning and ending time of Purnima are calculated astronomically and displayed in local civil time.

## Traditional interpretation

Many traditions attach specific observances to Purnima, with the particular practice depending on the lunar month, region and tradition.

## How should it be used?

Use the location-aware Tithi boundary when determining an observance. A festival associated with Purnima can have additional rules beyond simply asking whether the Moon looks full on a particular evening.

## Common misconceptions

### Purnima always occupies the entire civil date of the full Moon

No. The Tithi has its own start and end times.

### Every Purnima is the same religious observance

No. Different lunar months and traditions attach different observances to Purnima.
